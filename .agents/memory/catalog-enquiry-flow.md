---
name: Catalog enquiry flow
description: SKALA's combined product enquiry and no-payment requirement.
---

The SKALA catalog basket submits selected products together as one enquiry. It is not checkout: do not add online payment or describe a submitted enquiry as a confirmed purchase.

**Why:** The user chose one combined enquiry without online payment.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in basket submission, success messages, and owner-facing enquiry views.
