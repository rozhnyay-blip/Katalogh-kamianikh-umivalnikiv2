---
name: Catalog enquiry flow
description: SKALA's combined product enquiry and no-payment requirement.
---

The SKALA catalog basket submits selected products together as one enquiry. It is not checkout: do not add online payment or describe a submitted enquiry as a confirmed purchase. Enquiries do not reserve stock or decrement the owner-maintained availability count.

**Why:** The user chose one combined enquiry without online payment, so an enquiry is not a confirmed sale.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in basket submission, success messages, and owner-facing enquiry views; change stock counts only when the owner confirms an actual sale or stock change.
